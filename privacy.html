Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Operated by Beyond Muscles (Kelvin Edimeh)

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google or Email, we collect your name, email address, and optional fitness preferences (goals, experience level, equipment, weekly schedule, preferred foods, allergies, workout logs, weight entries, and daily check-ins) solely to personalize your training and nutrition experience.

2. Camera & Biometric Privacy

Camera frames captured in AI Form Check are used only to generate real-time posture feedback and are never sold or shared publicly. Fingerprint and face unlock verification is handled entirely by Android's native BiometricPrompt; Beyond Muscles never accesses or stores your biometric data.

3. Data Storage, Export & Deletion (NDPA 2023)

Your profile and logs are encrypted in transit and stored securely so your plan stays synced to your account. You may export all your data as JSON or permanently delete your account and stored data at any time from the You tab inside the app, or by emailing kelvinedimeh@gmail.com.

4. Sponsored Placements

Beyond Muscles is free for athletes and supported by clearly labeled sponsored offers from gyms and supplement brands. We never sell your personal health data to third-party data brokers.
